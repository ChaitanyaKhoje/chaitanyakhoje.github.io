# Phase 6: After Dark - UI Spec

## Visual Contract

The After Dark theme is cinematic, image-led, and restrained. It uses a near-black background, warm-gold accent, Newsreader italic display text, and low-contrast mono metadata. The overall feel is editorial and atmospheric rather than instrument-panel busy.

## Listing Contract

- Mast is minimal and anchored to the top edge.
- H1 is large, italic, and the dominant element in the first viewport.
- Stats row is compact, mono, and uppercase.
- Sessions are rendered as horizontal feed rows with a left index, central title block, thumbnail plate, and right meta columns.
- Thumbnails must be visible, not decorative placeholders.
- The page must read as a usable archive on both desktop and mobile.

## Post Contract

- Hero plate is wide and immersive, spanning the top of the content area.
- Title and coordinate / plate labels live on top of the hero or immediately adjacent.
- The prose column is the primary reading column.
- Sidenotes for apparatus and pipeline sit beside the prose in mono styling.
- The page footer is sparse and functional.

## Responsive Rules

- Listing feed rows stack or compress cleanly on narrow screens.
- Post two-column layout collapses to one column at mobile widths.
- Typography must remain within its containers without overflow.

## Exclusions

- No extra marketing or landing-page copy.
- No new navigation model beyond the current astro pages.
- No theme changes outside the astrophotography pages.
