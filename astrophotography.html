§ 03

Field Method

Pipeline overview
Acquisition
ZWO Seestar S50 (250mm f/4.8) in EQ mode with electronic field de-rotation. Short subs to control Bortle 7–8 sky glow.
Calibration
WBPP 3.0 in PixInsight — auto reference selection, local normalization, SNR-weighted stacking.
Processing
DBE → BlurXTerminator → NoiseXTerminator → StarXTerminator → statistical stretch → LHE → HDRMT.
Verification
FWHM / eccentricity / SNR scoring per frame. Drizzle ×1 to recover detail under the Seestar's pixel scale.
